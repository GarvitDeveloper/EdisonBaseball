The Program

Built for the
next pitch.

For more than two decades, Vinnie Abene has run Edison baseball on a simple idea: compete for every inning, develop every player, and let the scoreboard take care of itself.

The 2026 Eagles did exactly that. A 19-win season. A GMC Red title. A pitching staff that struck out 225 hitters and posted a 2.31 team ERA. And a senior class that leaves behind three Division I arms and a standard the underclassmen can see up close.

  • 01

    Pitching wins

    A 2.31 ERA, 225 strikeouts, and a rotation with three D-I signees. Connor Murphy's 0.54 ERA and 83 strikeouts led the way.

  • 02

    Pressure is a privilege

    One-run wins. A nine-inning walk-off. A no-hitter against St. Joseph. The 2026 season was decided in the small moments.

  • 03

    Next man up

    Twelve freshmen on the varsity roster. The future is already in the dugout, learning what it takes to wear the red and gold.

“I don't have 300 wins because of me, believe me. I've had some very good players.”

Vinnie Abene · Head Coach

The standard

“Diamond Jim” set the bar.

Jim Muldowney went 275–75 in 13 seasons at Edison, winning a pair of group championships and building the program into a state power. His name lives on in the GMC's Muldowney Tournament — and in every Eagles team that expects to compete for a title.

Home Field

The red turf
is a warning.

Mike Krychowecky Field opened in the summer of 2020 with scarlet turf, Vegas gold lines, new dugouts and a new scoreboard. It is one of the most distinctive home fields in New Jersey.

“We love the red field. It's unique to us. I kind of like the fact more that other people hate it than I love it.”

Vinnie Abene
